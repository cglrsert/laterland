Smart import

It reads them for you

Laterland uses Apple's on-device text recognition to understand every screenshot: what it is, where it is, and how long it takes.

  • Finds the title, the place, the link and the time it takes
  • Suggests what you saved it for, and asks when it isn't sure
  • Spots duplicates, and never deletes anything behind your back
  • Works in 8 languages, fully offline

Questions, answered

What does Laterland cost?

Laterland is a one-time purchase on the App Store. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. Every feature is included.

Do my screenshots get uploaded anywhere?

No. Screenshots are read on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition. Laterland has no servers and no account.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Is the island a game I have to keep up with?

No. There are no streaks, no timers and nothing to lose. The island simply grows when you do things, at your own pace.

What if I change phones?

Make a backup in Settings, keep it in iCloud Drive or Files, and restore it on your new iPhone.
