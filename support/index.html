How do I import my screenshots?

Tap the + button on Today and choose screenshots from Photos. You can pick hundreds at once.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. Make a backup in Settings and keep it in Files or iCloud Drive.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Make a backup in Settings, save it to iCloud Drive, then open it on the new iPhone with Laterland installed.

How do I remove the sample ideas?

Settings → Data → remove sample saves. Your own saves stay.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. Laterland follows your iPhone's language by default.
